QW to be played at Dreamhack again?

Saturday 12th January, 2008 | Skip to comments (18)

Dreamhack, the world’s largest computer festival, featured a QuakeWorld 1on1 tournament in their Winter 2007 event. Since it turned out to be quite successful, it seems that the event organisers have given QuakeWorld another chance to be part of the official tournaments. Dreamhack’s next event, scheduled to take place June 14th - 17th, is most likely going to have at least a 1on1 tournament for Quakeworld. Additionally, we have also received note about rumours concerning a possible 2on2 tournament to be held there as well.

With the lack of information, all of this is still kinda out in the open. However, we hope to get these rumours confirmed as soon as possible. Stay tuned!
