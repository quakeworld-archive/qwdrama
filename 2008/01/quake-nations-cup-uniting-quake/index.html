Quake Nations Cup, uniting Quake

Wednesday 9th January, 2008 | Skip to comments (70)

Quake.cz, the czech Quake fan site has started a new tournament called Quake Nations Cup which is a Quake dedicated championship across all Quake scenes.

The goal with Quake Nations Cup is to set cooperation between each scene and try to presents all games in the Quake series.

National teams are divided into squads for each game (up to two squads can be signed per game and nation). Each squad has a captain who is a part of a council that can update rules to be fair and correct.

As for now the map pool consists of DM2, DM3 and E1M2.

So far there are 21 nations and 37 squads signed up, check it out:
