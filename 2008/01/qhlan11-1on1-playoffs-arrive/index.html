QHLAN11: 1on1 playoffs and Grand Final finished

Friday 4th January, 2008 | Skip to comments (2)

The 2on2 tournament group stage is about to come to an end, which means that it is time for the 1on1 tournament playoffs. Players who qualified for the playoffs are the top two finishers of each group. The playoffs will be using single elimination system, which means that if you lose, you’re out. Read on to find out all the results of the playoff games and the grand final.
