DreamHack Summer is ON!

Thursday 12th June, 2008 | Skip to comments (2)

Despite the fact that DreamHack’s QW tournament was about to get canceled, it is very much happening right now. The tournament managed to attract staggering 16 competitors, which seems to be enough to please the organizers of this event. In the field of these 16 competitors we have some of the top names of QuakeWorld scene including the reigning champion, ParadokS.

Along with him there’s few familiar faces from the previous Dremhack tournament such as Bps, LocKtar, Nabbe, Riker and Inferno. With this lineup of players it seems like ParadokS might have good chances of getting some more loan from the Dreamhack. However in the mix of familiar faces, we also have one Dreamhack virgin who is no stranger to high level of QuakeWorld and that’s razor. Let’s see if he will be able to spice things up.
