QHLAN 11 has began!

Friday 4th January, 2008 | No Comments »

Late last night QHLAN11 started its first tournament, which was the 1on1 tournament. The players were split up into two divisions (Gold and Silver) depending on their skill level, gold for top dogs and silver for the rest. Due the amount of signups for this tournament the players had to be split again into four groups (A-D) in each division.

The deadline for group matches is Friday 14:00 CET but some players were quick enough to play all of their group games last night. However there is still plenty of games left even for some top players, so get your QTV settings right and find a match to watch from here.

Kurwa, Kurfal… What? Just, Krulowa Fal 2.0

Thursday 27th December, 2007 | 9 Comments »

Patryk Rzadzinski, who you may know better as Faustov in the QuakeWorld community, has released the latest version of his Krulowa Fal 2.0 project today. Krulowa Fal is supposed to make searching for specific players or browsing servers easier for the QuakeWorld players. The most radical change from version 1.0 is that Krulowa Fal 2.0 is now a standalone program running in the background. In cooperation with Quakeservers.net, searching with Krulowa Fal is now faster than ever before as the poller is linked live to the database.

NQR to announce shocking news for Season 11?

Friday 14th December, 2007 | 26 Comments »

Just recently NQR strengthened their admin crew by the addition of Molgrum who is pretty well known for running the custom map tournaments ”Free Thursday Kenya” and ”King of Tuesday Kenya”. With Molgrum aboard, people are starting to ask themselves whether or not this will also influence the map pool. Anyone who has followed the qw scene in the last year knows that the guy is a big fan of trying new maps - also for 4on4! Luckily the NQR crew consists of some oldschool players as well and we of course expect them to save the day, but what if they are fed up with the old maps too?
