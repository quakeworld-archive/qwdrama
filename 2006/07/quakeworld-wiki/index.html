QuakeWorld Wiki

Saturday 8th July, 2006 | Skip to comments (0)

qwdrama are proud to present a new website in the qwdrama network - The QuakeWorld Wiki.

The QuakeWorld wiki is open for anyone who want to contribute with information as long as it is QW-related. You must be registered to create and/or edit pages in the wiki. All pages are beeing checked and updated continuously, abuse results in a permanent ban from all current and future qwdrama websites.

We are looking for people who want to help administrate the wiki, so if you are interested visit #qwdrama and drop a message.

We will very soon launch another two sub-sites, information about these projects will be announced in a couple of days. So stay tuned folks!

Also, eizid has updated his column regarding the qw4ever tournament.
