Welcome to qwdrama!

Wednesday 7th June, 2006 | Skip to comments (0)

Taking off where QuakeWorld.nu left off (due to its breakdown), qwdrama aims at providing fresh news to the Quake community.

The qwdrama initiative is lead by XantoM and Xterm and has evolved during the last couple of months.

In addition to news, qwdrama also provides you with sought-after tutorials, high-class interviews as well as column updates.

And since today is the release date and everything, qwdrama will be generous enough to give you not just one, but two interviews you sure wouldn’t miss for the world!

If you think you are one such person - feel free to contact us by mail or visit our irc channel #qwdrama.

Long live QuakeWorld!
