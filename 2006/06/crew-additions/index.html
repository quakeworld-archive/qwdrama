Crew additions

Tuesday 13th June, 2006 | Skip to comments (0)

We have made two new additions to our crew.

stroller
You might recognize stroller from SD but he is also the vice president of Stockholm Lan Assosiation which is arranging QHLAN. He will within a couple of days start writing about QHLAN and give us a glimpse of its progress.

For those who want to know more about stroller and the next QHLAN read the interview with stroller.

eizid
eizid will provide with fresh coverage from the new qw4ever 4on4 and FFA tournaments.

We are still looking for people who can contribute with fresh qw material so don’t hesitate to contact us in #qwdrama or at contact@qwdrama.com.
