stroller: “more pr for QHLAN”

Monday 12th June, 2006 | Skip to comments (0)

Hello stroller! Tell us a little about yourself and your relation to QW and QHLAN.

My name is Martin Sundberg and im 19 years old. When I was 12 I went with lorns little brother to QHLAN 2 I think. and I was sitting in the kiosk and was trying to sell some soda. But I didnt really try QW then, it took 5 years until I tried and I liked it. Because of I fell in love with QW I wanted to work more on QHLAN and became the vice president of SLA (Stockholm Lan Association).

And after that I just went on working with it and helped lornelin more and more.

Were you satisfied with the last QHLAN? What went bad and what was good?

I think that it went okey. But the only thing that we were missing was the people. And what we learned from that was that we need to do more “pr” for QHLAN.

When and where will the next QHLAN take place?

We are not 100% sure, but we are talking to “Sollentuna sport och simhall” and I think we will be there. It’s the same place where “Reunion” where at. Its planned to take place in the begining of Januar.

Is there any special reason for your increased responsibility in this matter?

Yes because Jonas aka lornelin is making his last QHLAN. And that means that if we are making another QHLAN after this one somebody needs to know everything about arranging a good LAN and that would be me.

What are your expectations for the next QHLAN (participation, clans, big names)?

My expectations are that all top clans will be there, more then 150 people will come, great games and lots of fun.

That sounds like a very successfull LAN. Is there anything in particular that you want to tell us about the next event?

Yeah it is going to rock! Three div 1 clans have already said that they are comming and we are making T-SHIRTS! Plus XantoM is going to swimdive!

We will see about that =). Do you have any favourite QHLAN memory that you would like to share?

QHLAN 8 semi-final LA vs CMF, great games! And when we hade a big Suddendeath party at QHLAN 9.

You are going to run a QHLAN blog/column here at qwdrama. What are your thoughts about it?

It’s going to be great. I will post pics and write every news about QHLAN 10 so everyone will be updated about whats going on.

Ok! Anything else on your mind that you want to tell us?

Continue to follow everything on qwdrama, and start thinking about comming to QHLAN 10 it will be great!

Im sure it will be awsome. Thanks for your time, good luck with the QHLAN preparations and welcome to the qwdrama crew!

Thank you. Remember to bring back the roots! qwdrama 4 ever!
