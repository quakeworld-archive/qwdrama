EQL4 playoffs next week

Tuesday 14th November, 2006 | Skip to comments (0)

After soon 3 months and 190 played games the European Quake League season 4 is coming to an end. All teams have until the 19th of November (23:59 CET) to play and report their remaining games.

Teams who will reach playoffs (based on current standings):

Division 1 - koff, Slackers, Firing Squad, Legeartis

Division 2 - Team Freedom, Loser Squadron, Eläintarha, Osams

Division 3 - hippushnik, Easy To Kill, BaconMen, Rebellion of Thunder

Division 4 - ChoseN, Dies Ater, Morituri, Suddendeath.Project

Division 5 - The Pimps, Shadow Death, Def2, Clan Cube Squad

Division 6 - sadtroops, cool clan, RAW, Enemy Quad

Hopefully we will see some awsome games during playoffs and remember to post your scheduled games on the QW Schedule!
