QHLAN10 website launched

Thursday 9th November, 2006 | Skip to comments (0)

Last year the QHLAN website was launched in the last minute and it almost turned out as a disaster. This time the site is launched in good time and hopefully we will see far more attendees than QHLAN9.

QHLAN10 Official Website

For you that have forgotten, Stroller is writing about QHLAN and its progress in his QHLAN column.

If you pay before 1st of December you will only need to pay 150SEK thats about £11, €16 or $21. It might be important to know that the LAN is being held from the 4th until the 7th of January..

With that said, signup , take time off work and start planning your trip to QHLAN.
