Reppie joins cMF

Tuesday 13th March, 2007 | Skip to comments (12)

Yes, the dutch superstar player reppie, widely known for his faithfulness in clan Firing Squad, has just signed a contract with the Finnish superstar players fix and fifi in Clan MalFunction.

Signing the contract, reppie will be a welcome asset for cMF in the current season of NQR, as well as in the upcoming season of EQL.

This suddenly makes the current situation in the European QuakeWorld scene a bit more interesting now that playoff approaches and another new league season have begun.

Whether or not the clan Firing Squad will live on now that its former active players are being spread out in other clans, is unknown and will therefore be left unsaid.
