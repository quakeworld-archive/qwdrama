Ownage is progressing nicely and we got some pretty neat games coming up.

[1] Gamer vs doris
[2] lbn vs -insane-
[3] Mutilator vs ParadokS

1. Gamerii versus sudo. Both gamer and cs-judo has shown off some sweet action. Sudo has shown his place as a dueller beating Locktar 2-0 including a DM2 win, but Gamer will win both aerowalk and DM6 - 2-0

4. Ul up against Xalibur. This is so hard to predict. I dont know where to start. Xalibur showing up at QHLAN from a long break and putting up a nice show and to quote dag “I consider XalibuR a.k.a.”Dennis the Menace” to be a mad ass dueler.” UL on the other hand…. perfect aim and incredible movement.
