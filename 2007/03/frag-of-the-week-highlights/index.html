Frag of the week highlights

Monday 26th March, 2007 | Skip to comments (4)

Frag of the week competition has been running for 12 weeks now and there has been a total of 65 submissions showing plenty of awesome frags!

For those who hasn’t followed the weekly challenge, we would like to present the qwdrama crew’s favourite three Frag of The Week winners so far:

The competition is free for all and you don’t even have to be registered in order to participate in the competition. If you haven’t made a QuakeWorld video clip before you may want to take a look at our QuakeWorld Video Making tutorial. In case you are still unable to create your own video clip, you can send us your demo on IRC and we will turn it into video format.

Go gibbin’ and start submitting!
