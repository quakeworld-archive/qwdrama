Firing Sassa?

Wednesday 10th January, 2007 | Skip to comments (20)

Sassa has now officially departed with team Suddendeath and the rumors spread around that he is desperately trying to join the ranks of legendary Firing Squad. Sassa left Suddendeath because he wanted something fresh and according to the man himself being in Suddendeath felt like fucking same girl for two years.

Our sources also state that Sassa has already played few tryout games with Firing Squad and they did not go too well. Firing Squad has set up on internal poll and currently its results stand for fifty-fifty situation as Sassa has received two positive and negative votes.

We also have gained information that Sassa isn’t going to give up looking for new team even if the Firing Squad guys show him the way to the exit door. Next up he is going to contact either ToT or Freedom, whose members are mostly Swedish.
