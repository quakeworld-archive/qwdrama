QHLAN10 Wrapup

Sunday 7th January, 2007 | Skip to comments (4)

Somewhere in the early morning of Sunday 7th of January QHLAN10 finally came to an end. After gibbin’ each other for entire weekend without barely any sleep, the winners for 1v1, 2v2 and 4v4 tournaments were finally crowned. If you couldn’t stay up till 8 in the morning, you may want to see the demos of the grand finals which you can find right here:

  • 1on1 Finals: reppie vs interceptor
  • 2on2 Finals: Firing Squad vs Fragomatic
  • 4on4 Finals: Suddendeath vs Slackers

NOTE, the following content will feature the scores of all grand finals so if you do NOT want to know who won, don’t click on add comment or read more!


QHLAN 1v1 Tournament

Grand Final: reppie [3:1] interceptor

  • aero: 12:13 (OT)
  • dm4: 25:8
  • dm6: 23:10
  • ztn: 28:6

Demos: Download

Final Standings:

1) reppie
2) interceptor
3) xterm
4) ParadokS

QHLAN 2v2 Tournament

Grand Final: Firing Squad [3:0] Fragomatic

  • dm6: 75:53
  • aero: 106:54
  • dm2: 66:25

Demos: Download

Final Standings:

1) Firing Squad (reppie & razor)
2) Fragomatic (valla & xterm)
3) Slackers (ParadokS & murdoc)
4) Imba (bps & molecule)

QHLAN 4v4 Tournament

Grand Final: Slackers [3:1] Suddendeath

  • dm3: 167:137
  • dm2: 100:167
  • e1m2: 194:169
  • dm2: 171:156

Demos: Download

Final Standings:

1) Slackers (murdoc, krab, XalibuR, lascap)
2) Suddendeath (bps, ok98, molecule, mille)
3) Firing Squad (reppie, razor, serox, spoink)
4) Sassa’s Allstars (inter, sassa, ake vader, inferno)
