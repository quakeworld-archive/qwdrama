The return of NQR?

Wednesday 10th January, 2007 | Skip to comments (4)

Yesterday someone from the Nations Quake Rank crew slipped some information out to the public as their news bot printed a completely new message into their main IRC channel.

The message itself didn’t include anything special other than URL to season 10’s site. Although the site was just an empty template of NQR’s website it may still mean that the crew behind Nations Quake Rank is cooking up a brand new season.

This may be all false, but nonetheless we will keep you updated!

Update: The slipped information has become a fact. The NQR crew is indeed working on a new season and this was confirmed by konk, who is one of the admins for NQR. Konk also let us know that they currently don’t have everything they need to set up a new season but the work has started.
