qhdrama goes qwdrama again

Monday 8th January, 2007 | Skip to comments (10)

QHLAN is over but qwdrama has just restarted!

For all who didn’t attend to QHLAN, check out some of our coverage during the last days, pics.qhlan.org and demos.qhlan.org.

Now over to the Frag-of-the-week competition that has been running since last Monday. After a hard time comparing the clips (yes there are several good ones) we are happy to announce the winner of FOTW week 01: glt

This was the most popular by the judges (qwdrama crew) among the total of 9 submissions for FOTW week 01.

For this week we hope that more people will participate and give us more awsome frags, laughs and hyperskilled QW-moves.

For other youtube users submitting QW material; join the recently created QuakeWorld group on youtube and help us in creating a big QuakeWorld video archive.
