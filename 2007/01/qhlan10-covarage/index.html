QHLAN10 coverage

Wednesday 3rd January, 2007 | Skip to comments (0)

Tomorrow the 10th edition of the legendary QHLAN begins! The attendee list may not be as hot as we have seen in the past but I’m sure reppie and co will certainly put up a nice show.

Some quick information

  • Players signed up: 147
  • Nonswedish signed up: 38
  • Confirmed(paid): 69(41)
  • Countries represented: 11
  • Clans represented: 34 (based on signups)
  • Tournaments: 1on1, 2on2, 4on4, Wrestling, Secret tournament

We will try keep you as updated as we can on whats going on at QHLAN. Hopefully we will get some nice video footage that we can post.

And as for the last three QHLAN’s Sassa will be writing a QHLAN diary which he already have started on.

More reading, posts and updates can be found in the QHLAN forum and at the QHLAN10 offical website.

Note to the attendees: Please post your games on QW Schedule so that everyone can take part of the hot action!
